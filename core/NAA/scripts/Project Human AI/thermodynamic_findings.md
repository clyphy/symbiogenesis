## Key Concepts from Extropic's Thermodynamic Computing

*   **Thermodynamic Sampling Unit (TSU)**: A new type of hardware that performs sampling from programmable probability distributions rather than deterministic computations. This aligns with the ASDK's "stateless transform void" which operates as a pure performance engine.
*   **Energy-Based Models (EBMs)**: TSUs sample directly from EBMs, where the probability distribution is defined by an energy function. This provides a physical basis for the "White Buffalo Entropy" formula, where intelligence is viewed as minimizing informational entropy.
*   **Pbits (Probabilistic Bits)**: The fundamental building block of TSUs. Unlike classical bits (0 or 1), pbits output a random voltage that wanders between states, interpreted as a probability. This is a direct physical realization of the "quantum math substrate" and "thermal noise" concepts.
*   **Denoising Thermodynamic Models (DTMs)**: A new generative AI algorithm designed for TSUs that can be up to 10,000x more energy-efficient than modern deep learning on GPUs.
